02

What the test actually does

Costs first. Real spreads and commissions, in the units the venue charges them, before anything else is scored. Most claims stop here.

Your attempts are counted. The order form asks how many variations you tried before settling on this one, and the answer changes the bar. A test that ignores the search behind a rule is measuring the search, not the rule.

Placebos drawn from your own market. Hundreds of matched null draws built from the instrument being tested — not a generic random series, which is a softer opponent than the real thing.

A power floor that can refuse you. Under roughly 540 to 977 overlapping observations, depending on the strength being claimed, the test cannot answer and says so instead of guessing.

A holdout you never see. A slice of history is sealed on the day you submit and scored once. It cannot be re‑run, and neither can you.
